关于About

地理信息科学本科在读，坐标上海徐汇（31.1650°N、121.4050°E）。 关心城市里的冷与热：冷源在哪里、廊道在哪里断、修复该先从哪一段开始。

手上的两件事：一是上海市冷岛网络的跨期分析，用三期遥感影像和网络方法回答 「十年怎么变、变化来自哪、先修哪里」；二是一个已结题的大创项目， 用公开点评分析乌镇东栅与西栅的游客体验差异。

能做的

  • 遥感：Landsat 8/9 预处理、地表温度反演、植被与水体指数、土地覆被
  • 空间分析：MSPA、最小累积阻力、电路理论夹点、空间统计与网络连通性
  • 制图：专题制图、图集编排、用脚本批量出图（SVG / Pillow）
  • 工具：ArcGIS Pro（arcpy）、QGIS、Python、R、C++

Undergraduate in geographic information science, based in Xuhui, Shanghai (31.1650°N, 121.4050°E). I work on urban cold and heat: where cold sources are, where corridors break, and which segment to restore first.

Two things on hand. A cross-period analysis of Shanghai's cool-island network, using three periods of satellite imagery and network methods. And a concluded undergraduate project comparing visitor experience at Wuzhen's two gates from public reviews.

What I do

  • Remote sensing: Landsat 8/9 preprocessing, land surface temperature, vegetation and water indices, land cover
  • Spatial analysis: MSPA, minimum cumulative resistance, circuit-theory pinch points, spatial statistics
  • Cartography: thematic maps, atlas layout, scripted output (SVG / Pillow)
  • Tools: ArcGIS Pro (arcpy), QGIS, Python, R, C++
