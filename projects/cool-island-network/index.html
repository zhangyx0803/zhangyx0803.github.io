在回答什么

城市冷源（林地、草地、水体）靠景观连通性把降温服务送到周边建成区。上海十年间建设用地持续扩张， 冷源与热环境同步变化，但「网络怎么变、变化由什么驱动」还缺定量回答。

两个问题：

  1. 十年间冷岛网络的空间格局如何变化——源地、廊道数量与总长、连通性、重心。
  2. 变化主要由源地格局变动引起，还是由阻力面变动引起。

复现步骤

每一步都写明用哪个程序、产出什么。工具链是 ArcGIS Pro（arcpy）、Python（rasterio / numpy）、 GuidosToolbox 与 Circuitscape；仓库里带的是参数与脚本骨架。

  1. 建公共网格 — ArcGIS Pro，arcpy.management.CreateFishnet 生成覆盖全市的 30 m 全 0 栅格，之后所有输出都对它对齐。
  2. 裁剪与投影 — ArcGIS Pro，arcpy.management.Project 到 EPSG:4549；16 个区用 Dissolve 融合为市域。
  3. 反演地表温度 — Python 脚本（rasterio + numpy）：读 MTL 定标系数，QA 波段做云掩膜，输出 LST、NDVI、NDWI。
  4. 识别冷源 — ArcGIS Pro，Con / Raster Calculator：LST ≤ P30 且（NDVI ≥ 0.4 或 NDWI > 0）。
  5. 提取源地 — GuidosToolbox 2.8，MSPA（边缘 1 像元、八邻域），再按核心区 ≥ 0.1 km²、源地 ≥ 1 km² 过滤。
  6. 构建阻力面 — ArcGIS Pro，Weighted Sum：土地利用 0.40、不透水面 0.25、NDVI 0.20、距水体 0.15。
  7. 提取廊道 — ArcGIS Pro，Cost Connectivity：整数化路由面作成本栅格，源地作输入，输出最小生成树路径。
  8. 找瓶颈 — Circuitscape 5：成对模式算电流密度取夹点；高阻力段由廊道邻域阻力均值识别。
  9. 做归因 — Python 脚本：两期源地 × 两期阻力面四种组合，拆成源地效应、阻力效应与交互项。
  10. 排优先级 — Python 脚本：改善潜力 × 热环境紧迫性，熵权法定权，跨期用同一套权重比较。

Question

Urban cold sources — woodland, grassland, water — deliver cooling through landscape connectivity. Shanghai’s built-up area kept expanding over the decade, so sources and thermal environment changed together. Neither has been quantified in terms of how the network changed, or why.

Dataset Source Resolution Time
Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 Level-2 (SR + ST) USGS EarthExplorer, WRS-2 118/038 and 118/039 30 m (thermal native 100 m) Summer 2015 / 2020 / 2025, low cloud, same-day overpass

The study area is the whole municipality (including Chongming, Changxing and Hengsha), about 8,205 km², projected to CGCS2000 / 3-degree Gauss-Krüger, central meridian 120°E (EPSG:4549).

Data are not distributed with the repository. The imagery is bound by USGS terms and the intermediates are large. For reproduction, write to zhangyx0803@petalmail.com with your purpose.

Steps

  1. Build a common 30 m all-zero grid; align every output to it.
  2. Clip to the city boundary, project to EPSG:4549.
  3. Retrieve land surface temperature: MTL calibration, QA cloud mask, LST, NDVI, NDWI; guard against division by zero and clamp out-of-range indices.
  4. Identify cold sources: LST ≤ P30 and (NDVI ≥ 0.4 or NDWI > 0).
  5. Extract patches with GuidosToolbox MSPA (edge width 1, eight-neighbour), then filter: cores ≥ 0.1 km², sources ≥ 1 km².
  6. Build the resistance surface: land use 0.40, impervious 0.25, NDVI 0.20, distance to water 0.15; derive a routing surface and a land-only surface from it.
  7. Extract corridors: integer cost raster plus source patches, minimum spanning tree paths.
  8. Locate bottlenecks: high-resistance corridor segments, Circuitscape pinch points in pairwise mode, barrier segments on the land surface.
  9. Attribution: two source periods × two resistance surfaces, split into source effect, resistance effect and interaction.
  10. Rank priorities: improvement potential × thermal urgency, entropy weights, identical weights across periods.

Status

Completed: problem definition, data inventory, method chain and parameters, a 12-step handbook. Ongoing: imagery ingestion and LST retrieval. Not started: patches, resistance surfaces, corridors, bottlenecks, attribution. Dated progress is listed at the bottom of this page.
